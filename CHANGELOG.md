# Changelog

## 0.1.4

- Recruit cards show the game’s role and attack-style icons when there is room for them.
- Recruit highlights follow mouse hover or controller focus. An unavailable Buy action leaves its card unhighlighted.
- Updated the Human and Undead recruitment screenshots.

## 0.1.3

Clarify the mod’s description and compatibility for players.

## 0.1.2

- Weekly recruit cards show the unit’s localized role and attack style above its quote when space permits.
- A bright outline marks the focused recruitment action. At 1.5× UI scale the native keyword panel leaves the rightmost card and rank badge visible.
- Inspecting a unit keeps Choose and Cancel clear of the stat sheet. Back returns focus to the inspected recruit or its affordable Buy button.

## 0.1.0

- Added repeat Hermit recruitment visits every seven days after the native Day 2 encounter in standard runs. Challenges keep their authored schedule.
- Repeat visits use the active recruitment pool, respect banishes, and wait for other visitors to finish.
